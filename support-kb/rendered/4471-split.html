The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. Reports arrive by app and email, and by booking-v2’s form, whose hint says DD/MM/YYYY.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type the month first. Its hint says MM/DD/YYYY, then the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only tenants on booking-v3, only through the form. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs the right hint for the tenant’s locale.

shift: the day moves by one, not month and day. “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday”, “every booking in June is one day off”, “the invite arrived before I made the booking”. That is a time zone or daylight-saving bug, not date order. The desk had already read these reports as a one-day move (desk notes). The table counts them where they are filed, under parser, and again on their own row.

How it got merged

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line. “Wrong day on the invite” fits all three problems, so all three landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags by the reporter’s browser, not by the tenant and form. The tags in the table disagree with the cause in both directions; go by tenant and channel.

For the desk

  • Route new date reports by tenant, form and channel (#lib/kb/causes), not by subject or tag.
  • A report that says “one day off” is shift, whatever its subject. Add its id to oneDayMove in desk notes.
